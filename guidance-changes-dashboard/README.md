# Guidance changes: who raised, cut or narrowed this season, and the long-term targets still standing

Every guidance figure a large SEC filer published since 1 July 2026, lined up against the figure it published last time: the funnel from "said something" to "moved something", raises against cuts by size, sector and metric, the named revisions with both figures quoted, the narrowed ranges, the multi-year targets in force, and the guard rails (moves inside the prior range, "bigger is worse" metrics, pairs the rules refuse to compare). Built on FocusAlpha's `get_guidance_changes` and `get_guidance`, with `screen_companies` choosing the universe.

- **[index.html](index.html)** — the sample as published at [app.focusalpha.ai/guidance-changes-dashboard](https://app.focusalpha.ai/guidance-changes-dashboard/). 716 companies (SEC filers worth $10bn or more that filed an earnings release since 1 July 2026), every change row in the window and every standing multi-year target, all **as of 2026-09-24**; it never updates. The sector, size and company controls narrow the page in the browser.
- **[live.html](live.html)** — the same page with no data baked in. It reads everything through the viewer's FocusAlpha connector: one `screen_companies` call for the universe, then `get_guidance_changes` and `get_guidance` (`view: "latest"`) per company. Publish it as a claude.ai artifact with `capabilities: { mcp: { servers: [{ server: "FocusAlpha", tools: ["screen_companies", "get_guidance_changes", "get_guidance"] }] } }`.
- **Where to edit:** the three blocks at the top of the script — `WINDOW` (the season), `SCREEN` (the universe, in `screen_companies` parameters) and `MAX_COMPANIES` (the page reads two tools per company; 150 is a comfortable default for one sitting).

Things this dataset will punish you for getting wrong: a raise is a number going up, not good news — rows with `higher_is_worse` (capex, tax rate, costs) are shown as recorded and flagged; direction comes from the company's own words, never from the arithmetic, and a trimmed range is "narrow", not a cut; pairs the comparability rules refuse are absent, not guessed at; a cut beyond about 25% deserves a look at the source, because a company that separated a business mid-year compares a larger business with a smaller one; long-term targets are the newest standing figure per metric, so an old target counts until the company replaces it.
