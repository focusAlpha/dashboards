Replicating portfolio

Trading only on what had been filed

get_market_dataget_benchmark_prices

Each book is bought at the close of the first trading day after its 13F appeared on EDGAR and held until the next filing — buying at quarter-end would use information nobody had yet. Long US-listed equity only: no shorts, options or non-US listings, so this is not the fund's own performance.
